# 01 — Operational Automation

## Problem

Routine infrastructure tasks become inconsistent when they rely on memory or repeated manual execution.

## Simple Explanation

Automate predictable, low-ambiguity work and leave a record.

**Validate → Execute → Log → Review**

## Practical Pattern

Good candidates include inventory, scheduled maintenance, account administration, data export and repeatable health checks.

## Evidence Produced

Execution logs, reports, exception outputs and scheduled-task history.

## Caveats

Do not automate unclear business decisions merely because they are technically possible.

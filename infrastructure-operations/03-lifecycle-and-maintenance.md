# 03 — Lifecycle and Maintenance

## Problem

Infrastructure risk increases when assets remain in service without clear ownership, maintenance or retirement planning.

## Simple Explanation

**Know it → Own it → Maintain it → Replace it → Retire it**

Track support status, maintenance windows, dependencies and end-of-life dates.

## Evidence Produced

Asset records, maintenance history, lifecycle plans and decommission evidence.

## Caveats

A technically functioning system may still be unsupported or operationally risky.

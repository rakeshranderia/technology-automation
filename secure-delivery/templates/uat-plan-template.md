# User Acceptance Testing Plan

## Purpose

Define how the business will confirm that the solution meets agreed requirements and is suitable for operational use.

## Scope

### In Scope

- key business processes;
- critical user journeys;
- integrations;
- reporting;
- data outcomes.

### Out of Scope

Clearly identify excluded areas.

## Participants

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Business Owner | Overall acceptance |
| UAT Lead | Coordinates testing |
| Testers | Execute business scenarios |
| Technology Team | Supports defects and remediation |
| Vendor | Supports product-specific issues where applicable |

## Entry Criteria

UAT begins when:

- technical testing is complete;
- critical defects are resolved;
- environment is available;
- test data is prepared;
- UAT scripts are approved.

## Test Cycles

For complex implementations, use multiple cycles:

**Cycle 1 → Remediation → Cycle 2 → Final Acceptance**

## Defect Classification

- Critical — blocks business operation
- High — significant impact, no acceptable workaround
- Medium — manageable impact
- Low — cosmetic or minor

## Exit Criteria

UAT is complete when:

- agreed scenarios pass;
- critical/high defects are resolved or explicitly accepted;
- known limitations are documented;
- business owner approves release.

## Evidence

- completed UAT scripts;
- defect register;
- sign-off;
- accepted limitations;
- release decision.

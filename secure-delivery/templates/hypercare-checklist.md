# Hypercare Checklist

## Purpose
Provide a defined stabilisation period after go-live.

## Before Hypercare
- [ ] Start/end dates defined
- [ ] Support contacts confirmed
- [ ] Escalation path confirmed
- [ ] Monitoring confirmed
- [ ] Known issues documented
- [ ] Vendor support available where required
- [ ] Business contacts identified

## During Hypercare
- [ ] Incidents tracked
- [ ] Defects separated from enhancements
- [ ] Workarounds recorded
- [ ] User feedback captured
- [ ] Performance monitored
- [ ] Integrations monitored
- [ ] Data issues reviewed
- [ ] Review cadence established

## Exit Criteria
- incident volume stable;
- critical defects resolved;
- known issues owned;
- support team can operate independently;
- monitoring effective;
- documentation current;
- business owner accepts transition.

## Evidence
- issue log;
- status summaries;
- defect register;
- support handover;
- exit approval.

## Principle

**Hypercare is a transition phase, not a substitute for a sustainable BAU support model.**

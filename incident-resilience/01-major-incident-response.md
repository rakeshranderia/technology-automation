# 01 — Major Incident Response

## Problem

During a major incident, technical teams can focus so heavily on restoration that ownership, communications and decision-making become fragmented.

## Simple Explanation

Incident response needs both technical action and coordinated control.

**Detect → Triage → Contain → Communicate → Recover → Close**

## Practical Pattern

Confirm incident lead, technical leads, business impact, affected services, communication cadence and decision authority.

Maintain a working incident record containing:

- timeline;
- impact;
- actions;
- owners;
- decisions;
- workarounds;
- recovery status.

## Evidence Produced

- incident timeline;
- communication record;
- technical actions;
- recovery evidence;
- decisions and exceptions.

## Control Alignment

Supports incident management, operational resilience, logging, communications and recovery objectives.

## Caveats

Fast restoration should not eliminate evidence or allow temporary fixes to become permanent without review.

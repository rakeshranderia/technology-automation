# 02 — Disaster Recovery Validation

## Problem

A documented recovery plan does not prove that systems can actually be recovered within expected timeframes.

## Simple Explanation

Recovery capability needs to be exercised.

**Plan → Simulate → Recover → Measure → Validate → Improve**

## Practical Pattern

Define:

- services in scope;
- recovery objectives;
- dependencies;
- recovery sequence;
- owners;
- success criteria.

Run simulations or recovery exercises and compare actual results with expected recovery targets.

## Evidence Produced

- exercise plan;
- recovery timings;
- issues discovered;
- dependency gaps;
- improvement actions;
- updated procedures.

## Control Alignment

Supports disaster recovery, business continuity, resilience testing and continual improvement.

## Caveats

A successful infrastructure recovery does not automatically prove the business process is recoverable end-to-end.

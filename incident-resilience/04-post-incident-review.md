# 04 — Post-Incident Review

## Problem

Incidents repeat when recovery ends without learning.

## Simple Explanation

A post-incident review should improve the system, not assign blame.

**What happened → Why → What worked → What failed → What changes**

## Practical Pattern

Review:

- timeline;
- root/contributing causes;
- monitoring;
- communications;
- recovery;
- dependencies;
- documentation;
- control gaps.

Assign actions with owners and due dates.

## Evidence Produced

- PIR;
- action register;
- updated runbooks;
- control improvements;
- closure evidence.

## Caveats

Root cause should not be reduced to the final technical failure if organisational or process factors contributed.

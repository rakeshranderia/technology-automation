# Incident Communications Cadence

## Suggested Structure

### Initial Notification
Send once material impact is confirmed.

Include:
- affected service
- business impact
- investigation underway
- next update time

### Ongoing Updates
Use a predictable cadence based on severity.

Each update should contain:
- current impact
- confirmed facts
- actions underway
- workaround
- decisions required
- next update time

### Recovery Notification
Confirm:
- service restored
- validation completed
- monitoring period
- known residual issues

### Closure
Confirm:
- incident closed
- PIR planned if required
- follow-up actions owned

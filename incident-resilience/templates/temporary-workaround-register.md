## Review Questions

- Is the workaround still required?
- Has risk increased?
- Are compensating controls still effective?
- Is the permanent fix funded and scheduled?
- Has the workaround become operational dependency?
- Should formal risk acceptance be renewed?

## Principle

**A temporary workaround should have an owner, a review date and a path to closure.**

# 03 — Workarounds and Recovery Risk

## Problem

Temporary workarounds introduced during incidents can quietly become permanent operating practice.

## Simple Explanation

A workaround is a risk treatment for a limited period, not automatically a permanent design.

**Workaround → Owner → Expiry → Risk → Permanent Fix**

## Practical Pattern

Record:

- why the workaround exists;
- affected service;
- owner;
- risk;
- expiry/review date;
- permanent remediation.

## Evidence Produced

- workaround register;
- risk acceptance;
- review date;
- closure/remediation evidence.

## Control Alignment

Supports risk management, change control, resilience and technical-debt management.

## Caveats

A workaround without an owner and review date is likely to become unmanaged BAU.
